## How It Was Merged
Final table is monthly (`MS`). Water-level records are deduplicated by (station_name, date), canonicalized, and one primary target station is selected by coverage/completeness score. Exogenous precipitation/temperature/runoff are aggregated across stations by date (means/sums/counts) and outer-joined on `date` to a complete monthly timeline. Calendar, lag, rolling, anomaly, and event-proxy columns are then added.
